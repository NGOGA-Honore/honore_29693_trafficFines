## How the research shapes the design

- Sources 1 and 2 show that unpaid fines and repeat offenders are common, which justifies the arrears report and the repeat-offender function.
- Sources 4 and 5 show that a 30-day deadline and a late fee already exist in Rwanda, so the fine table has a due date (issue date + 30 days) and a surcharge that is configurable because the amount is not published.
- Source 5 shows paid fines disappear from the driver's view, so my payment table keeps full history and an audit log.
- Source 3 shows a one-year window in the proposed point system, so the repeat-offender function counts violations over the last 12 months.
- Sources 6 and 7 show the dataset has violations but no fines or payments, so fines and payments are generated by my own PL/SQL rules.
